About Us

Our History

BrightTech Solutions began in 2014 as a small computer repair shop in Accra, started by two friends who believed that good technology should be affordable and easy to use. Word spread quickly, and soon schools, shops and offices were coming to us for more than repairs.

Today we are a full IT company. We sell hardware and software, design and install networks, and provide ongoing support for customers across Greater Accra.

Milestones

  1. 2014: Opened our first repair shop in Accra.
  2. 2017: Began selling computers, accessories and software.
  3. 2020: Launched network installation and cabling services.
  4. 2023: Introduced monthly IT support plans for businesses.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, affordable technology products and friendly IT support that help individuals and businesses work better every day.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted IT solutions company in Ghana, known for honest advice, quality service and customers who succeed with technology.
